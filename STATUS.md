## Current decision

Use a **hybrid typed procedural architecture**:

1. curated shader primitives and families;
2. typed AST or graph generation;
3. cryptographically strong seeds;
4. browser compilation and validation;
5. local candidate search and visual-quality gates;
6. structural and perceptual non-repetition;
7. exact export templates;
8. optional lightweight browser model operating only on the constrained representation.

## Immediate work

## Claims

### Defensible now

- Generation can run entirely client-side without a paid service.
- Exact seed collisions can be made negligibly probable with cryptographically strong 128-bit seeds.
- Local repetition can be actively reduced.
- Global perceptual uniqueness cannot be guaranteed without shared global state.

### Not defensible now

- Every shader is globally unique.
- A perceptually similar shader can never appear on another device.
- A small browser LLM will reliably generate visually excellent, portable GLSL.
- The current proof of concept is the final artwork.
