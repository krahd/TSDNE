# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.2 — 2026-08-04

### Artistic and research framing

- centred the project on **one object, no return, take the source**;
- reframed the title as a deliberate contradiction: the shader exists as executable process;
- replaced the former “personal without personalisation” thesis with an open empirical question;
- distinguished interface withdrawal from universal deletion;
- moved local AI from principal claim to optional, testable mechanism;
- rebuilt the related-work argument around withdrawal, taking, persistence, and executable circulation;
- added an internal audit-response register and AI-use disclosure.

### Implementation

- renamed the documented representation from typed AST to bounded family program;
- removed hidden authored fallback mutations attributed to WebLLM;
- reject ineffective model proposals;
- permit bounded family changes in model proposals;
- record explicit proposal provenance;
- added random and authored mutation baselines;
- made local AI opt-in and disabled by default;
- disclosed the pinned remote WebLLM runtime and external model requests;
- seek four valid candidates and select by recent distance and family coverage;
- separated broad temporal descriptors from dense high-frequency luminance-change screening;
- added synthetic tests for flashing sequences, stable sequences, empty proposals, provenance, and baselines.

### Documentation and licensing

- corrected Demokritos author order;
- separated the two ShaderGPT projects;
- separated the ISMAR 2025, arXiv 2026, and CHI EA 2026 ShadAR records;
- added Chun, Kirschenbaum, Gonzalez-Torres, and Sehgal as central lineages;
- described the local encounter record as a bounded behavioural trace rather than denying the category;
- foregrounded GPL as the substantive free-software commitment;
- limited CC0 language to waivable rights that may exist;
- changed project status from release candidate to alpha;
- replaced journal-first completion language with visual, public, empirical, and accessibility gates.
