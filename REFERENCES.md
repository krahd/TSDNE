## 14. Citation controls

Before using this index in a manuscript:

1. replace repository/project descriptions with complete bibliographic citations where available;
2. verify all preprint publication statuses;
3. verify current platform and model licences;
4. preserve access dates for mutable web documentation;
5. do not infer legal ownership or public-domain status from technical availability;
6. do not convert absence from this search into a priority claim;
7. keep technical infrastructure sources separate from artistic precedents;
8. distinguish encounter-time generation from pregenerated delivery;
9. distinguish local inference from offline operation or total privacy;
10. distinguish computational identity from perceptual uniqueness.
