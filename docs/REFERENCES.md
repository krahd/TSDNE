## 15. Citation controls

1. Treat this file as a source index, not proof that each work has been read in full.
2. Prefer canonical proceedings and publisher records over later preprint metadata for publication facts.
3. Preserve preprints as supplementary locators only when they add access or version information.
4. Distinguish separate versions, authorship, and feature sets.
5. Recheck mutable repositories, services, browser support, models, licences, and policies at manuscript freeze.
6. Do not infer legal ownership or public-domain status from technical availability.
7. Do not convert absence from this search into a priority claim.
8. Distinguish artistic precedents, technical infrastructure, implementation neighbours, and political counter-models.
9. Distinguish local inference from offline operation, privacy, provenance, or low energy use.
10. Distinguish computational identity, perceptual resemblance, and encounter identity.
11. Read every source used in a submitted manuscript in the cited form.
12. Verify author order directly, especially for collaborators’ work.
