## Citation controls

1. Prefer published proceedings or publisher records over later preprint metadata.
2. Preserve preprints as supplementary locators when useful.
3. Recheck mutable platforms, repositories, browser support, models, and licences at manuscript freeze.
4. Do not infer ownership or public-domain status from technical availability.
5. Do not convert absence from this search into a priority claim.
6. Distinguish artistic precedents, technical infrastructure, implementation neighbours, and political counter-models.
7. Distinguish encounter-time generation from pregenerated delivery.
8. Distinguish local inference from offline operation or total privacy.
9. Distinguish computational identity from perceptual uniqueness.
10. Read every source included in a submitted manuscript in its cited form.
