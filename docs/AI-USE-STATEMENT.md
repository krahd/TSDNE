# AI and computational-method statement

## Public artwork

The public version of *This Shader Does Not Exist* does not use a language model, diffusion model, remote inference service, or third-party generative API.

Its visual output is produced by authored procedural programs executed locally in the visitor’s browser. Candidate selection uses deterministic and stochastic computational techniques, perceptual measurement, technical validation, and novelty-oriented search.

## Why this matters

The absence of a foundation model is not a claim that procedural systems are immaterial, neutral, or free of infrastructure. The artwork still depends on browsers, hardware, electricity, hosting, standards, and prior mathematical and software traditions.

The narrower claim is that TSDNE does not require:

- a scraped training corpus;
- an opaque checkpoint;
- remote model hosting;
- inference logging;
- a high-memory model runtime;
- unresolved model-output provenance;
- behavioural data from the visitor.

This makes the work’s privacy, preservation, licensing, and device-access position substantially clearer.

## Relation to artificial intelligence

TSDNE nevertheless enters the cultural field of artificial intelligence. Its title invokes the contemporary genre of synthetic entities that “do not exist,” and the work addresses conditions widely associated with generative AI:

- inexhaustible plausible output;
- automated production;
- uncertain authorship;
- abundance without prior inventory;
- the instability of originality and ownership;
- the difference between computational and perceptual identity.

The project contests the equation of generativity with foundation models. Its refusal is part of the artwork.

## Non-LLM computational search

The system generates several candidate programs and evaluates them locally. It uses:

- authored generative grammars;
- deterministic pseudo-randomness from cryptographic seeds;
- WebGL2 rendering;
- perceptual descriptors;
- quality and safety constraints;
- maximin novelty selection;
- bounded local anti-repetition memory.

These methods do not infer the visitor’s preferences and do not construct a behavioural model.

## Future non-LLM methods

The following may be explored only when they improve the visual work and preserve the encounter:

- reaction–diffusion;
- cellular automata;
- neural cellular automata trained on wholly controlled material;
- compositional pattern-producing networks;
- small authored neural dynamical systems;
- evolutionary and quality-diversity search;
- coupled oscillators and reservoir systems.

Their inclusion would not be advertised as novelty or as a generic claim of “AI.” Each method would require a separate audit of training material, reproducibility, compute, licensing, preservation, and device access.

## Development assistance

Project documentation and software development may use contemporary programming assistants during development. Such assistance does not run in the public artwork. All committed code, concepts, claims, references, licences, and artistic decisions remain subject to the author’s review and responsibility.
