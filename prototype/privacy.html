Privacy

TSDNE has no account, analytics, advertising, behavioural profile, or server-side inference. Shader generation and optional model inference occur in the browser.

A bounded history of encounter identifiers and compact visual descriptors is stored locally to reduce obvious repetition. It is not uploaded and can be cleared from about.

The local model is downloaded from its external model host and cached by the browser. Ordinary web infrastructure may receive standard HTTP metadata such as IP address and user agent.
