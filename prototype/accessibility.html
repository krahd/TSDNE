Accessibility and visual safety

The artwork provides pause, reduced-motion, and reduced-intensity controls. It respects the system reduced-motion preference and suppresses the long local-AI transition in reduced-motion mode.

Keyboard: N another, U use, Space pause/play, A about.

Automated candidate checks reject extreme temporal differences and high flash ratios, but the project does not claim clinical seizure safety. Visitors sensitive to animation should enable reduced motion or pause immediately.
