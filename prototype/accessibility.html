Accessibility and visual safety

The artwork provides pause, reduced-motion, and reduced-intensity controls. It respects the system reduced-motion preference, removes long transitions in reduced-motion mode, and does not require WebGPU or a model download.

Keyboard: N another, U use, Space pause/play, A about.

Automated screening

Candidates are checked using broad temporal descriptors and a separate dense, low-resolution screen for repeated large-area luminance transitions. The dense screen is designed to reject obvious high-frequency full-screen or large-area flashing that sparse samples could miss.

This is a conservative automated heuristic. It is not a medical assessment, a Harding test, WCAG certification, or a guarantee across every display, browser, refresh rate, and visual pattern.

Guidance

Visitors sensitive to animation should enable system reduced motion before opening the work where possible, use pause immediately, and enable reduced intensity through about.

External photosensitive-content analysis and assistive-technology review remain required before release.
